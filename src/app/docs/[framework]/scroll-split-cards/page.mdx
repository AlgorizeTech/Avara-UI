# Scroll Split Cards

A cinematic scroll-driven storytelling component featuring a unified panoramic landscape that splits into three distinct physical card panels upon scrolling. As the user continues downward, the cards separate, execute a perspective-rich 180° 3D flip to reveal curated editorial narratives, and finally float upward away from the viewport.

`ScrollSplitCards` brings tactile visual immersion to brand origin stories, architectural journeys, editorial photography features, and landmark case studies.

Under <code>prefers-reduced-motion</code> the panels track the input directly.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="p-6 rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card">
    <h3 className="text-lg font-semibold text-card-foreground mb-2">Dual-Sided 3D Cardstock</h3>
    <p className="text-muted-foreground text-sm">Each panel has genuine 3D card faces with <code>preserve-3d</code> and <code>backface-visibility</code>, flipping 180° to reveal typographic narratives with individual color schemes.</p>

## Best Practices & Guidelines

<div className="flex flex-col gap-4 my-6 not-prose">
  <div className="rounded-xl border border-foreground/[0.06] bg-foreground/[0.03] p-4">

    <p className="text-[13px] leading-relaxed text-foreground/50">Place <code>ScrollSplitCards</code> in a standard vertical flow without conflicting parent <code>overflow-hidden</code> constraints. Both mousewheel scroll and touch drag gestures naturally advance the animation through each stage.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="rounded-xl border border-foreground/[0.06] bg-foreground/[0.03] p-4">
    <h4 className="font-sans text-[14px] font-semibold text-foreground mb-1">Panoramic Image Selection</h4>
    <p className="text-[13px] leading-relaxed text-foreground/50">For the most dramatic visual impact, use wide landscape photography (recommended aspect ratio 21:9 or ~2.4:1) with a resolution of at least 2400×1000px. High-contrast horizon or architectural imagery splits cleanly across the panels.</p>
  </div>
  <div className="rounded-xl border border-foreground/[0.06] bg-foreground/[0.03] p-4">
    <h4 className="font-sans text-[14px] font-semibold text-foreground mb-1">Editorial Card Content & Legibility</h4>
    <p className="text-[13px] leading-relaxed text-foreground/50">Keep narrative copy concise: 1–2 sentences per card along with an optional metric callout and kicker. Contrast-tested background colors ensure text remains easily legible on all viewports.</p>
  </div>
</div>
