# Component Wall

A landing hero for a component library: the copy on the left — a changelog pill, a bold headline, a paragraph, two actions and a line of trust — and on the right a wall of screenshots that never ends. A camera hangs over the wall, zoomed in on one card; every few seconds it pulls back, glides to the next card, and settles on it, brightening that card while the rest stay dimmed.

The wall is an <DocLink to="image-wall">Image Wall</DocLink>: one tile of cards in staggered columns, repeated around itself eight times, so however far the camera travels it never finds an edge. Every screenshot sits in the same 493:332 frame, and the camera walks the cards in order — each one gets its turn before any comes round again. This hero only positions it — the whole right side on desktop, fading under the copy; a band under the copy below that. The faces are an <DocLink to="avatar-stack">AvatarStack</DocLink> and the stars are <DocLink to="half-stars">HalfStars</DocLink>.
