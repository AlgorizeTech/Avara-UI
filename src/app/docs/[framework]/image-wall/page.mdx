# Image Wall

A wall of screenshots that never ends, and a camera hanging over it. The camera rests zoomed in on one card; every few seconds it pulls back, glides to the next card and settles on it, brightening that card while the rest stay dimmed. Hovering the wall holds the camera where it is.

The wall is one tile of cards in staggered columns, repeated around itself eight times, so however far the camera travels it never finds an edge. Every image sits in the same 493:332 frame under a small mono caption, and the camera walks the cards in order — each gets its turn before any comes round again. It fills whatever box you give it: a tall window shows one card at a time, a short band eases the zoom down so a few show at once, and on a phone it pulls back further still so the flight between cards is something to watch. <DocLink to="component-wall">Component Wall</DocLink> is this wall beside a column of hero copy.
